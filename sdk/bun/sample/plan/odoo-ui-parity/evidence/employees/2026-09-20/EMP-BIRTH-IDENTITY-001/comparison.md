# Comparison and blockers

Both authenticated surfaces expose Personal Information at desktop and mobile
sizes. Core3 renders the exact new labels, while its deterministic fixture
values are correctly hidden by the active-company guard. Odoo renders Place of
Birth and Gender, but this installed reference does not render the exact
Country of Birth label; it renders Nationality (Country) in citizenship
instead. This is recorded as a parity blocker, not treated as a pass.

The Odoo shell also emits seven known app-icon 404s; no page errors occurred.
