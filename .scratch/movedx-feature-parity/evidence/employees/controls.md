# Employee Directory Checklist

## Visible controls

- Employee list supports search, status tabs, CSV export, column chooser, and CRUD actions.
- Employee detail shows profile/status, contracts, activity timeline, and uploaded documents.
- HR writers can edit profile data and upload documents; readers can download permitted documents.

## Interaction checks

- Filter active, leave, and inactive employees and verify counts/rows.
- Open employee `employee-01` and verify contract and activity sources.
- Upload a document, confirm it appears in the document grid, then download it.
- Edit employee profile and confirm the list/detail refresh and activity event.
- Reject document uploads without an employee or outside the size limit.
